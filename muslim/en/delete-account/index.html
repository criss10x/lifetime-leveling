Delete Account

Local data

Uninstalling the app removes progress that is stored only locally on your device.

Delete an account backup

Email us, preferably from the Google account used for backup, with the exact subject or phrase: Delete my Muslim Leveling account. An email request removes the account and all server-side backup data within seven days. muslim.leveling@gmail.com.

If you never signed in

If you never signed in with Google, progress stays only on your device and there is no server backup to delete.
