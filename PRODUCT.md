## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro + TypeScript is the recommended static-site stack for GitHub-to-Hostinger deployment; final selection is confirmed during the implementation-plan review.

## Users

- People discovering Lifetime Leveling and its applications, including prospective Android users.
- Muslim young adults who want to build consistent worship habits through a motivating, practical daily routine.

## Product Purpose

Lifetime Leveling is the parent product studio for focused mobile applications. Its first public product, Muslim Leveling, turns daily worship into a supportive progression system: people complete worship quests, see their progress, and return with more consistency.

## Positioning

Muslim Leveling combines prayer-time-aware worship quests with XP, levels, streaks, achievements, and learning in one Android app. It works local-first; Google Sign-In is optional and exists only to back up and sync progress.

## Operating Context

- Lifetime Leveling is a bilingual public web presence, deployed from GitHub to Hostinger.
- The studio domain will use `lifetimeleveling.com`; Muslim Leveling will use `muslim.lifetimeleveling.com`.
- Google Cloud Platform is used for OAuth/Google Sign-In and Search Console verification, not website hosting.
- The first release is Android-only and uses a direct Google Play download call to action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Muslim Leveling includes prayer quests (mandatory and sunnah), tilawah, sedekah, dzikir, XP, levels, streaks, achievements, cosmetics, prayer schedules and reminders, Quran reading and murottal, learning content, and quizzes.
- The website must link to clear privacy, terms, support, and account-deletion pages for store and OAuth needs.
- Google Sign-In must never be presented as a requirement to use the app.
- iOS distribution is intentionally out of scope for this first release.

## Brand Commitments

- Copy is bilingual: Bahasa Indonesia and English.

## Product Principles

1. Make a visitor understand the app's real daily value before asking them to download.
2. Treat consistency as supported progress, never pressure or guilt.
3. Keep product facts and privacy practices precise enough for Google verification.
4. Design mobile-first, with Android as the only current distribution channel.
5. Let each app keep its own product identity while the studio provides a recognizable parent brand.
